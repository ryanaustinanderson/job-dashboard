After a call — your notes, saved to the person

So the next conversation starts where the last one left off:

  1. Right after the call, send Muse a photo of your notes — or just type or say what you remember.
  2. Muse summarises them into the post-call template below and attaches them to that person.
  3. Before the next conversation, tap that person anywhere on this page — their contact details, prior-call notes, and next step are all in the panel, ready to review.

The template Muse fills in for every call:

PersonName, company, role
DateWhen you talked
How we talkedCoffee in person / phone / Zoom / LinkedIn message — where, how long
What they said2–4 bullets: their advice, what their team does, what they look for, names they mentioned
What I promisedAnything you said you’d send or do — resume, intro, follow-up, article
Next stepThe one concrete next thing, and who does it
Date to follow upDefault 3 business days, then 7 (the 3B7 routine)
Relationship noteAnything personal to remember — shared school, city, hobby, something they mentioned

One rule: nothing gets invented. If you don’t remember what someone said, the note says “not sure — check.”
